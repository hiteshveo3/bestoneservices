Service overview

Prepare the property for its next stage.

Moving out involves deadlines, keys, inspections and a long list of practical jobs. A focused end of tenancy clean helps bring the property back to a clear, presentable condition before handover.

Every property is different. A studio flat, family home, furnished rental and shared accommodation will not need exactly the same approach. That is why the enquiry starts with the property rather than a fixed one-size-fits-all package.

Handover thinking

A calmer handover starts with clear expectations.

The clean should be planned around the condition of the property, its contents, access arrangements and the deadline that matters. That is more useful than presenting every customer with the same fixed list.

Clear scope protects both sides. It helps the customer understand what is being discussed and gives the service team the information needed to respond properly.
