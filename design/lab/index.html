Ornament and type

Six ornaments taken from the trade itself. Keep the ones you like; the system will use two or three at most.

S01

Hallmarks

Four small punches carry four facts, the way a British hallmark records maker, standard, office and year. For proof points and document headers.

S02

Floor plans

The language of lettings. A simple plan shows the fix, never the pest: sealed entry points, rooms cleaned, boxes per room. For illustrations, reports and landlord pages.

S03

Uniform piping

The lime piping on the uniform traces one seam of a card. The only line the system allows, because it is ornament, not a divider.

S04

Four petals

The logo's four petals, one per service, as a quiet corner mark or a bullet. Echoes the logo without copying its colours.

S05

The streak

The mark a touchstone leaves. Once per screen, under the one word that matters.

S06

The inventory clerk's stamp. For done states, reports and the end of a booking.
