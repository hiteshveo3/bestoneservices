The quietest of the three. Pale stone page, charcoal text, and lime only where something passes or needs a tap. It reads premium and unhurried, which suits landlords and agents.

The closest to today's site and to the uniform. White page, today's deep olive text, and lime used generously for bands, plaques and blocks. The most energetic, and the easiest move from the current build.

Built like a job sheet. Warm paper, charcoal text, the tallest headings and big figures. The four hallmark marks sit in the hero and records replace cards. Dense and trustworthy, strongest for agents managing many properties.
